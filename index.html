Frequently Asked Questions

Cyber security, cloud, support & business queries answered

Q: Do small businesses really need cyber security?
A: More than ever. Over 40% of cyber attacks target small businesses because they are easier targets. Prevention is far cheaper than recovery.
Q: How long does a security audit take?
A: A full audit usually takes 1–3 business days depending on the size and complexity of your IT infrastructure.
Q: We already have antivirus software. Is that enough?
A: No. Antivirus is only one layer. Real protection requires endpoint security, network security, email filtering, and monitoring systems.
Q: Can you help after a cyber attack?
A: Yes. We provide incident response including containment, investigation, recovery, and full system hardening.
Q: Will security implementation disrupt operations?
A: No. We implement solutions in phases or off-business hours to ensure minimal or zero disruption.
Q: Do we need full cloud migration immediately?
A: No. We recommend a phased approach starting with non-critical systems first.
Q: Which cloud platforms do you support?
A: We work with AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud depending on business requirements.
Q: Is cloud data really secure?
A: Yes. We use encryption, IAM controls, and compliance-grade security frameworks.
Q: Do you provide training?
A: Yes. We provide onboarding, security awareness training, and periodic refresh sessions.
Q: Can I get a demo before buying?
A: Absolutely. We offer a free live demo tailored to your business needs.
